Scientific Findings & Limitations Summary

1. Trace Heterogeneity Insights

Empirical analysis of the CRAWDAD PAM'09 mesh dataset indicates pronounced SNR and noise floor variation across 5 GHz and 2.4 GHz bands. However, frame counts in trace captures reflect snapshot samples, and cannot be interpreted as channel airtime load or medium utilization.

2. Explainable Channel Ranking

The channel ranking model uses bounded score anchors across physical SNR, noise floor, stability, and transmission rates. Small sample sizes are accounted for through explicit confidence interval bounds. Weights represent user or policy preference and are marked ASSUMED.

3. CSMA/CA MAC Modeling & Bianchi Validation

The discrete-event DCF engine simulates contending wireless stations under IEEE 802.11 standards. Under saturated, error-free conditions, simulation results closely match Bianchi's 2000 analytical model within predictable Monte Carlo confidence bounds.
